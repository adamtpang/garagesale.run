### Shipping does not match the board

The draft used to say "buyer pays calculated shipping." The surplus board subtracts $25 of shipping from the profit, which only makes sense if Adam pays it. Both cannot be true.

- If Adam pays shipping, the board's math stands, and the listing should say free shipping.
- If the buyer pays, the board is about $25 too low on profit, and buyers will weigh the shipping cost against the price.

The $25 is an estimate, not a quote. Adam picks one before posting, and the board's shipping line is changed to match.
