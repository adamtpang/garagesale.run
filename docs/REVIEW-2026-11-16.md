## Roles
- **Fable builds.** Fix the problems below, then build what the pilot needs (see "What the pilot needs").
- **Flipping pilots.** Every day it scans Public Surplus, GovDeals, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, eBay and OfferUp, prices each candidate against real sold comps, and brings Adam the best flips with links, the all-in cost, the estimated resale, the risk and a max bid.
- **Adam decides.** No bid, buy, offer, seller message or published listing happens without Adam's yes on that item. No spending. No merge or deploy without Adam's ask.

## Problems to fix, most serious first

1. **High: the surplus board doesn't apply Adam's rules.** In `web/lib/surplus.js` (about lines 284-291), a lot becomes a buy candidate once its confirmed net is above $0. Nothing checks the $100 minimum profit, the $100 max cost or the radius, and distance only shows up as risk text. The 52-mile TFC lot shows "needs preview" and the 73-mile Hallettsville lots show "watch." Fix: make the rules hard checks, read from one config, that turn a lot to "skip" and give the reason.
2. **High: the Zebra TC700K lot's $210.39 estimate is too optimistic.**
   - It's costed at today's $10 bid instead of a realistic winning bid.
   - Resale is the median of six sold comps that mix single units and lots, and some had batteries. These 8 have no batteries.
   - It counts one $25 box and one order fee, so it's treated as a lot sale while being priced from single-unit sales.
   - The listing draft's own fallback is $200 for the lot.
   Fix: show a low, middle and high case. Subtract battery cost when batteries are missing. Separate lot comps from single-unit comps. Show profit at a computed max bid, not at the current bid.
3. **High: PR #7 (the Price Tag redesign) made parts of the site hard to read, and it's live.**
   - The assistant panel `.asst` has near-black text on dark grey (`globals.css` about line 127), and so do the suggestion chips `.chips2` (about line 132). They show on every page through `layout.js`.
   - The `.unsure` warning is pale pink `#ffb4ae` on white (about line 281).
   - The chat bubbles and stepper dots Bugbot flagged are also unfixed.
   Fix them all for the white theme, then screenshot every page on desktop and mobile before merging.
4. **Medium: PR #12 ignores a typed price cap.** In `web/lib/pricing.js` (about line 28), the check for "in" as inches also matches the word "in", so `parseRequest("Nintendo Switch under $200 in Austin")` returns `max: null`. Also, games sold "with extras" can slip into console comps (about line 50). Bugbot flagged both after the merge. Fix both and add tests for the cases above.
5. **Medium: process.** Every PR from #6 to #12 merged within minutes, often before Bugbot finished, and its findings were left open. Every merge to main deploys to production, but the PR bodies say "Not deployed." From now on:
   - Open the PR, wait for Bugbot, and fix or answer every finding.
   - Don't merge. Adam merges.
   - Describe deploy status accurately.
6. **Medium: no preview builds.** `web/vercel.json` skips builds when nothing in `web/` changed, which is correct. But every preview deployment shows as canceled, so design changes can't be checked before they're live. Fix: allow preview builds for PRs that touch `web/`.
7. **Low: the S20 FE resale is a picked median.** It's $68 from 12 comps out of 35 found, because `cleanLot` keeps only the first 12 (`slice(0,12)`). Use all of them, keep working and for-parts phones separate, and flag a likely device-management or carrier lock on fleet phones.
8. **Low: the Zebra listing draft claims more than we know.** "Normal wear," "ships within 2 business days" and "photos show all 8" aren't verified, and the photos don't exist yet. "Buyer pays shipping" contradicts the $25 shipping the board subtracts. Mark these as fill-ins until they're checked.
9. **Low: missing data and stale claims.**
   - Cash register lots 4123508 and 4123506 have no close time or buyer premium.
   - `BUY_SIDE_TEST.md` claims a Nov 8 live check of logic written Nov 15.
   - The #12 PR body says 56 tests, but there are 59.

## What the pilot needs (build order)
1. **One rules config** (budget, minimum profit, radius, categories, gas cost per mile, and the optional lot and ROI rules, off by default) that the board, the buy search and the API all read.
2. **A working write path for Flipping.** Today `POST /api/surplus` and `POST /api/deals` reject the current `GST_WORKER_KEY` with a 401, and `GET /api/scout/config` returns all nulls. Fix the auth so a rotated key works, and document the request shape. Adam sets the new key in Vercel and gives it to Flipping as a secret. Never commit it.
3. **A daily deals feed.** Each deal includes:
   - The source URL, site, location and distance.
   - The current bid or price, the close time, and the buyer premium, tax, fees and gas.
   - The all-in cost, and the resale value as low, middle and high cases, with each sold comp's URL and sale date.
   - Profit and ROI, a max bid, and the risks.
   - A decision of buy, watch or skip, with the reasons.
   Unknown values stay "unknown", never guessed.
4. **The board shows the buy side and the sell side next to each other.** Where to buy the lot, on one side. Where its units sell, on the other marketplace, with sold-comp links.
5. **A ledger of decisions.** Record what Adam approved, bought, sold and earned, using the encrypted ledger from PR #2.
6. Tests for each rule and each fee calculation. Fix one item at a time in small PRs.
